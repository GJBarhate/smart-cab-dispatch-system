## 1. What this competition actually is

"Ideas of India" Season 2 is an ET (Economic Times/ET Edge ecosystem) innovation challenge hosted on TechGig. Key facts from the challenge page: 3 rounds, team size 1–4, 577 registrations, prize pool worth ₹2,00,000, ends 07 Oct 2026. The current round is **document-based idea shortlisting** — no live pitch yet. That means: **the application document is 100% of your Round-1 score.** The organizers say it explicitly: *"The jury highly recommends reviewing the submitted document as it forms a critical part of the evaluation process."*

Realistic math: ~577 registrations across all domains, likely 60–150 actual submissions in this domain (many registrants never submit). A rigorously researched, quantified, honest document with a real prototype typically lands in the top 10–15% of such pools — most submissions are generic, unquantified, and have no demo. Your competition is weaker than it looks. (No one can promise 100% shortlisting — but you can control every scoring lever.)

## 2. How the 100 marks really work — and how to farm each parameter

| Review parameter | How to score high |
|---|---|
| Problem-Solution Fit | Open Q1 with a regulatory deadline + a quantified victim population. Solution must map 1:1 to the stated pain, not "climate" in general. |
| Innovation & Uniqueness | NAME the existing alternatives and state your white space in one line. Juries reward candidates who know competitors. |
| Prototype Readiness | Claim "Functional Prototype" honestly, describe the working stack, state what's NOT built. Honesty reads as maturity. |
| Scalability & Sustainability | Show a revenue model + a replication mechanism (config-driven engine, cluster-by-cluster GTM). |
| Env. & Societal Impact | Conservative arithmetic shown step-by-step beats big round numbers. Include social (jobs, inclusion), not just tCO₂e. |
| National Priority Alignment | Name-drop precisely: CCTS, Net Zero 2070, NDC/Panchamrit, Mission LiFE, Viksit Bharat 2047, SDG 7/9/12/13. This parameter is free marks that most applicants leave on the table. |
| Presentation & Communication | Word limits respected, Calibri 12, zero extra questions, clean tables, a deck with diagrams, a 2-min demo video. |

## 3. What type of ideas get accepted (winning-pattern research)

Patterns extracted from Smart India Hackathon winner analyses, Startup India National Awards (climate winners like Pump Academy), ENTICE clean-energy challenge, IIT-B–Honeywell Sustainability Hackathon, and India climate-tech funding trends (Tracxn: $2.6B in 2025; winners like Alt Carbon, Aurassure, Battery Smart, Chakr Innovation):

1. **Regulation-timed ideas win.** Solutions riding a live policy deadline (CCTS compliance, CBAM) score "national alignment" automatically.
2. **Measurable > impressive.** Judges reward a defensible "5–12% energy savings, X tCO₂e measured" over "revolutionary AI platform."
3. **Working demo is the great separator.** "PowerPoint-only presentations receive low marks" — recurring theme in SIH judge feedback.
4. **Inclusivity is a scoring multiplier** in India-government-flavored juries: vernacular, MSME/rural, women-led onboarding.
5. **Root-cause focus.** Winners solve *why the problem persists* (cost + language + expertise barriers), not the symptom.
6. **Student teams win by honesty + prototype**, not fake traction. "Pre-launch, validated on simulated data, LOI pipeline" is a winning maturity story.
7. **Over-submitted generic ideas get rejected**: generic waste-segregation apps, generic EV-charging finders, generic carbon-footprint calculators for individuals, tree-planting apps, generic smart-bin IoT. Your idea must visibly not be one of these — B2B compliance MRV for MSMEs is not.

### Why this exact idea has the best acceptance profile
- **Live regulatory forcing function:** CCTS compliance in force FY2025-26 for ~490 entities in 7 sectors (aluminium, cement, chlor-alkali, pulp & paper, refining, petrochemicals, textiles), scaling to ~740 entities/9 sectors, 700+ Mt CO₂e covered; first compliance filing due **31 July 2026**; penalty 2× CCC price; first credit trading mid-2026. Obligated giants will squeeze their MSME suppliers for emissions data — and MSMEs have nothing.
- **CBAM second squeeze:** EU carbon border tax hits Indian exporters (steel, aluminium, cement) — export-linked MSMEs need carbon numbers to keep orders.
- **Documented software gap:** enterprise tools are expensive/English/months-to-deploy; Indian entrants (sentra.world, Greenio, Zoei, IIM-B tool) exist but none owns *vernacular + AI-ingestion + CCTS-native + MSME price point* — that's your differentiation sentence, and knowing these names scores "Innovation & Uniqueness."
- **Market tailwind for the jury's "scalability" lens:** India carbon-accounting software market projected to ~$4.5B by 2033 at ~29% CAGR (Grand View Research).
- **You can genuinely build the prototype in days:** bill upload → LLM extraction → emission-factor math → dashboard → PDF report. It's your PeerCode/FlowShield stack re-aimed.
