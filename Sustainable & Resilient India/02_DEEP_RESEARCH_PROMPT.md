## MISSION

You are an elite research analyst preparing intelligence for a national innovation-challenge submission in India ("Ideas of India" Season 2 — Sustainable & Resilient India domain, ET/TechGig, judged on: Problem-Solution Fit, Innovation & Uniqueness, Prototype Readiness, Scalability & Sustainability, Environmental & Societal Impact, Alignment with National Sustainability Priorities, Presentation). Conduct EXHAUSTIVE web research and produce a structured dossier. Rules: every claim needs a source URL + year; prefer primary/official sources; flag anything uncertain; India-specific data always beats global data; recent (2024–2026) beats old. Do not summarize superficially — extract exact numbers, names, dates, prices.

## RESEARCH TRACK A — The Competition Itself
1. Find everything about "Ideas of India" (ET Edge / Economic Times / TechGig innovation challenge): Season 1 — which ideas were shortlisted/won, what profiles won (students vs startups), jury composition, sponsors. Search: `"Ideas of India" ET Edge winners`, `TechGig innovation challenge sustainable resilient india shortlist`, `site:et-edge.com ideas of india`, `site:techgig.com ideas of india`.
2. Decode the evaluation: for each of the 7 review parameters, infer from similar Indian competitions (Smart India Hackathon, Startup India National Awards, ENTICE, IIT-B Honeywell Sustainability Hackathon) what a 9/10 answer looks like vs a 6/10. Produce a "what the jury rewards" cheat-sheet with evidence.

## RESEARCH TRACK B — The Regulatory Goldmine (CCTS & CBAM)
3. India Carbon Credit Trading Scheme: current obligated sectors + entity counts, GEI targets FY25-26/FY26-27, Form A deadline(s), penalty formula, ICM portal, ACVA accreditation, offset mechanism status, first CCC trading date. Sources: BEE (beeindia.gov.in), Ministry of Power notifications, PIB, ICAP, IETA India brief, CEEW, IEEFA.
4. EU CBAM current status for Indian exporters: definitive regime timeline, covered sectors, estimated cost impact on Indian steel/aluminium/cement exports (₹ or % figures), Indian government response. Sources: European Commission, GTRI reports, Exim Bank.
5. PAT scheme → CCTS transition history (shows you understand policy lineage).

## RESEARCH TRACK C — The Victim & The Market (MSMEs)
6. MSME hard numbers: count (Udyam registrations), GDP share, export share, employment; % with any sustainability/energy staff; energy cost as % of MSME operating cost by cluster type. Sources: msme.gov.in annual report, Udyam dashboard, CII/FICCI/SIDBI surveys.
7. Cluster archetypes: pick 3 real clusters (e.g., Tirupur knitwear, Morbi ceramics, Ludhiana engineering) — units count, typical fuel/energy profile, any BEE/UNIDO/GEF energy-efficiency cluster study with measured savings %.
8. Cost of current solutions: consultant fees for a GHG assessment in India, pricing of enterprise carbon software, pricing of Indian tools (sentra.world, Greenio, Zoei ESG, StepChange, Breathe ESG, IIM-B free tool) — build a price/feature comparison table.

## RESEARCH TRACK D — Competitive & Technical
9. Full competitive matrix: global (Persefoni, Watershed, Sweep, Normative, Plan A) + Indian players — target segment, pricing, languages, CCTS-native?, MSME-focus?, AI document ingestion? Identify precisely the unoccupied white space.
10. Technical building blocks: India-specific emission factors (CEA grid emission factor latest value, IPCC 2006/2019 factors, India GHG Program factors), GHG Protocol Scope 1/2/3 method, what a CCTS Form A actually requires, digital MRV best practices (World Bank/UNFCCC digital MRV papers).
11. AI feasibility evidence: examples of LLM/OCR document-extraction accuracy for invoices/utility bills; any published India pilots.

## RESEARCH TRACK E — Impact Math & National Alignment
12. Defensible impact assumptions: tCO₂e per typical MSME unit by cluster type; realistic %-savings from measurement+efficiency (BEE SME programme results); ICM/CCC expected price band; green jobs multipliers (SCGJ - Skill Council for Green Jobs data).
13. National mission map: Net Zero 2070, updated NDC (45% emission intensity ↓ 2030, 50% non-fossil), Panchamrit, Mission LiFE, National Green Hydrogen Mission, RAMP scheme for MSMEs, ONDC/DPI analogy for open MRV rails, SDG 7/9/12/13 — one line each on how an MSME carbon platform serves it.
14. Any government scheme that could FUND or adopt this (RAMP, MeitY startup schemes, BEE programmes, State climate funds) — names + what they offer.

## RESEARCH TRACK F — Winning Patterns (idea acceptance intelligence)
15. Analyze 10+ winning submissions/pitches from comparable Indian sustainability challenges: common structure, how they present impact numbers, how student teams compensate for no revenue, how much weight working demos carried. Extract a 10-rule "winning submission playbook."
16. Red flags that get submissions rejected: overclaiming, vague impact, no prototype, copy-paste generic ideas (list the 10 most over-submitted sustainability hackathon ideas to make sure we differentiate from them).

## OUTPUT FORMAT (strict)
Produce the dossier as:
1. **Executive brief** (1 page): the 10 facts that will most strengthen the application.
2. **Fact bank table**: ≥ 40 rows — [# | Fact | Exact figure | Year | Source name | URL | Use in form question Q1–Q9].
3. **Competitive matrix table.**
4. **Jury cheat-sheet** (per review parameter: what 9/10 looks like).
5. **Winning playbook** (10 rules) + **rejection red-flag list**.
6. **Impact calculation walkthrough** (show arithmetic for beneficiaries, tCO₂e, cost savings — conservative/moderate/ambitious scenarios).
7. **Gaps & risks**: anything that could undermine the chosen idea (existing identical product? policy change?) with mitigation.
8. **Source log**: every URL visited, grouped by track.

Be exhaustive. Depth wins. Begin now.
